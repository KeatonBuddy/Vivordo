# Vivordo Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 1, 2026**

## Introduction

Vivordo ("Vivordo," "we," "us," or "our") is a wellness application focused on stress, recovery, fitness, and performance. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how and why we use it, when we disclose it, how long we keep it, and the choices available to you.

This Policy applies to Vivordo users in the United States, Canada, and other jurisdictions where Vivordo is available. Some features are optional and collect information only after you enable the feature and grant the requested permission.

Vivordo is not a health-care provider or insurer, and the service is not intended to provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Laws such as HIPAA or provincial health-information laws may not apply to Vivordo in every circumstance. Regardless, we treat health and fitness information as sensitive personal information.

For privacy questions or requests, contact **contact.vivordo@gmail.com**.

## Information We Collect

### Account information

We collect information needed to create and manage your account, such as your name, email address, authentication identifiers, and account settings. Authentication is provided through Firebase Authentication and supported sign-in providers. Vivordo does not receive your password when authentication is handled by one of those providers.

### Profile and body information

During onboarding and in your profile, we ask for your first name, year of birth, and sex (you can choose "prefer not to say"), and optionally your height, weight, and body-fat percentage. Vivordo uses your age, sex, height, and weight to compare your cardio fitness with typical values for people like you and, when no measured value is available, to estimate your VO₂ max for the Physical Health score. You can change this information in Fitness → Body.

### Health and fitness information

With your permission, Vivordo may collect or import health and fitness information from Apple Health, Google Health and Fitbit, WHOOP, supported Bluetooth wearables, and information you enter or measure in Vivordo. Depending on the source and the permissions you grant, this may include:

- heart rate, resting heart rate, and heart-rate history;
- heart-rate variability and related cardiovascular measurements;
- blood oxygen (SpO₂) and breathing rate;
- cardio fitness (VO₂ max);
- sleep duration, sleep timing, sleep stages, sleep efficiency, and sleep-performance information;
- steps, distance, floors climbed, exercise time, workouts, active energy, and related activity information;
- weight and other body measurements; and
- source, measurement, and synchronization timestamps.

Vivordo uses this information to display your measurements, calculate user-facing wellness and stress features, identify trends, personalize recommendations, and generate insights you request or enable. Derived information—such as the scores described in "Scores and the burnout check" below—is Vivordo output and may be based partly on information imported from a connected service.

### WHOOP information

If you connect your WHOOP account, Vivordo currently requests permission to access WHOOP sleep and recovery information and offline access so that authorized synchronization can continue without requiring you to sign in each time. Imported sleep information may include sleep duration, start and end times, sleep stages, sleep efficiency, sleep consistency, sleep-performance percentage, time in bed, and related timestamps. Imported recovery information is limited to resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, blood oxygen, and breathing rate; Vivordo does not store WHOOP's own Recovery or Strain scores. Vivordo uses this information to display your history and to calculate Vivordo scores, the burnout check, and Vivordo Insights.

Vivordo may also receive live heart-rate readings directly from a WHOOP device through Bluetooth when you intentionally pair the device and permit Bluetooth access. Bluetooth heart-rate readings do not come from the WHOOP cloud API.

Vivordo does not currently use the WHOOP cycle, workout, profile, or body-measurement API scopes. If we introduce a feature requiring additional WHOOP information, we will update our disclosures and request your authorization before accessing it.

### Fitbit and Google Health information

If you connect Fitbit through Google Health, Vivordo requests read-only access to activity and fitness information, health metrics and measurements, and sleep information. Depending on availability, Vivordo may import Fitbit-sourced distance, floors climbed, active calories, heart-rate summaries, daily resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, blood oxygen, breathing rate, cardio fitness (VO₂ max), weight, sleep duration, sleep timing, sleep stages, and sleep efficiency. Vivordo uses this information only to provide and improve visible health, fitness, trend, score, and insight features in the app.

Vivordo does not write health or fitness information back to Fitbit or Google Health through this connection. We request only the permissions needed for enabled features.

Vivordo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements. Google API information is not sold, used for advertising, or used to determine eligibility for credit, insurance, employment, housing, or health care.

### Apple Health information

If you enable Apple Health access, Vivordo reads only the categories you authorize, which may include Cardio Fitness (VO₂ max) recorded by an Apple Watch. Vivordo only reads Apple Health information; it does not write to Apple Health. You can review or revoke those permissions in Apple Health or your device settings. Apple Health information is not used for advertising or sold to data brokers.

### Camera-based pulse measurements

If you use Vivordo's fingertip pulse scan, the app uses the camera and flash during the scan to estimate a pulse waveform and calculate measurements such as heart rate and heart-rate variability. These measurements are estimates and are not medical-grade. Vivordo does not intentionally retain raw camera images or video from the scan; it stores the calculated measurements and related quality or timestamp information needed to provide the feature.

### Information you provide

We collect information you choose to provide in check-ins, questionnaires, goals, journals, chats, support requests, social or circle features, and other app features. This may include mood, perceived stress, energy, notes, priorities, journal entries, messages, profile information, and content you share with other users.

The optional morning check-in asks how you feel and how you slept. Vivordo stores your answers with that day's information, saves your "how do you feel" answer as a mood check-in, and uses both answers in the Capacity and Physical Health scores.

Onboarding asks when you usually finish your main work or classes for the day. Vivordo stores this time and uses it to tell after-hours activity apart from your working day. Earlier versions of onboarding asked a set of work and stress questions; Vivordo no longer asks them, and answers you gave before are kept until you delete them or your account.

### Calendar information

If you connect Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar, Vivordo may access calendar names, event titles, descriptions, locations, attendees, start and end times, availability, and identifiers needed to synchronize changes. Vivordo uses calendar information to show your schedule, estimate availability or workload, provide schedule-related insights, and—when you choose an applicable action—create, edit, or delete calendar events. Vivordo does not modify an event without a user-initiated action or another clearly disclosed feature you enable.

### Daily records for Your Day

To calculate how demanding your day is (Demand) and what it took (Effort), the app saves a daily record to your Vivordo account. It contains each calendar event's start and end times and how demanding it is, your priorities' times, effort, expected duration, and when you completed them, and your end-of-day time. It does not contain event or priority titles, notes, or calendar event identifiers.

To keep these records complete, Vivordo stores your device's notification token together with the hour that is 11 PM in your device's time zone. Each night, Vivordo sends a silent notification at that hour, which you do not see, so the app can update the day's record in the background.

### AI interaction and insight information

We collect prompts, chat messages, answers, feedback, and generated responses when you use AI-assisted features. To provide personalized responses, Vivordo may send the content you submit and the minimum relevant account, health, fitness, journal, or calendar context to AI service providers acting on our behalf, which currently may include Anthropic and Google. These providers process the information to return the requested feature output and under their applicable service terms and data-processing commitments.

Vivordo AI uses Anthropic's Claude and asks for your consent before sending anything. That consent covers chat and the sorting of your calendar events and priorities:

- **Sorting calendar events.** When Vivordo can't tell how demanding an event is, it sends the event's title, duration, and number of attendees—never its description or notes—through Vivordo's servers to Anthropic, which rates the event so Demand and Effort are accurate. Ratings are kept on your device so the same event isn't sent again.
- **Estimating priorities.** When you leave a priority's effort or duration blank, Vivordo sends only the priority's title to Anthropic to estimate them. Estimates are saved with the priority and labelled as estimated; values you enter yourself are never replaced.
- **Remembering context.** Vivordo AI saves short facts you share in chat—such as what stresses you, what helps, patterns you notice, life context, and preferences—and a short summary of each conversation. It sends them with later messages so it doesn't lose track of what you've told it. Nothing is saved from a conversation about a crisis. Saved facts and summaries are deleted when you delete your account, or on request.

If you don't give consent, Vivordo AI is unavailable and events and priorities are not sent to Anthropic. You can withdraw consent in Profile under Vivordo AI.

Vivordo does not sell personal information or use WHOOP, Fitbit, Google Health, Google Calendar, Apple Health, or other sensitive health information to train a generalized advertising model. We do not permit service providers to use this information for targeted advertising. Where required, Vivordo will obtain additional consent before using sensitive information for a materially different AI purpose.

### Device, diagnostics, and usage information

We may collect device type, operating-system version, app version, language, time zone, approximate region derived from technical information, app-specific identifiers, feature interactions, diagnostic logs, crash information, performance information, and push-notification tokens. We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Vivordo. We do not use health information for targeted advertising.

### Scores and the burnout check

Vivordo's servers calculate scores from the information described above and store them, with the ingredients behind them, in your account:

- **Capacity**—the energy you have today—from sleep, overnight heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, your morning check-in, and how demanding your recent days were;
- **Effort**—what your day took—from your daily records, in-app workouts (type and duration), exercise minutes, and active calories;
- **Physical Health**—your longer-term fitness and habits—from active minutes, steps, strength workouts, cardio fitness (VO₂ max, measured or estimated from your age, sex, height, weight, activity, and resting heart rate), and sleep; and
- **the burnout check**—a nightly comparison of your last two weeks of Capacity, Effort, and mood with your own usual levels, using sleep, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, back-to-back events, and after-hours time to explain the result. When a warning starts, Vivordo may send you one notification.

These are wellness signals, not medical assessments or diagnoses.

- provide the features you request and synchronize connected services;
- display health, fitness, sleep, calendar, journal, and activity information;
- calculate and explain Vivordo stress, Capacity, Demand, Effort, Physical Health, heart-health, activity, and related scores, and run the burnout check;
- personalize insights, recommendations, reminders, and AI-assisted responses;
- send notifications you've allowed, such as check-in reminders and burnout warnings, and silent notifications that keep your daily records up to date;
- support social or sharing features you intentionally use;
- respond to support, privacy, and security requests;
- diagnose errors, monitor performance, prevent abuse, and protect Vivordo and its users;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements; and
- improve Vivordo using appropriately protected information and internal analyses consistent with this Policy and applicable platform rules.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not use WHOOP Data, Fitbit Data, Google API information, Apple Health information, or other sensitive health information for targeted advertising. We do not use such information to make decisions about credit, insurance, employment, housing, or eligibility for health care.

## Legal Bases and Consent

Depending on your location and the feature involved, we process information with your consent, to provide a service you request, to comply with law, or for a legitimate interest such as security and service reliability that does not override your rights. We request separate device or account authorization before accessing sensitive sources such as health data, calendars, Bluetooth devices, notifications, the camera, or connected wearable accounts.

You can withdraw permission through Vivordo, the connected service, or your device settings. Withdrawing permission stops future access but does not automatically remove information that was previously imported unless this Policy or the applicable control says otherwise.

## How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information in these limited circumstances:

### Service providers

We use providers that process information on our behalf to operate Vivordo, including Google Firebase and Google Cloud for authentication, databases, hosting, functions, analytics, and app reliability, and Anthropic or Google for enabled AI features (including, with your AI consent, sorting calendar events and estimating priorities as described above). We provide only information reasonably necessary for the relevant service and require providers to protect it through applicable agreements and controls.

### Connected services

When you connect or interact with Apple, Google, Fitbit, WHOOP, Microsoft, or another service, information is exchanged as needed to authenticate the connection, retrieve information you authorized, perform an action you requested, revoke access, or maintain synchronization. Those services process information under their own privacy policies.

### Other users and user-directed sharing

Information is shared with other users only when you intentionally use a sharing, circle, challenge, messaging, or similar feature and as shown in that feature. Do not include sensitive information in content you do not want recipients to see.

### Legal, safety, and corporate events

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, protect rights and safety, investigate fraud or abuse, or respond to a security incident. Information may be transferred as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law and any consent required by platform policies.

### Aggregated or de-identified information

We may use or disclose information that has been aggregated or de-identified so it cannot reasonably identify you, where permitted by law and applicable connected-service terms. We do not sell Fitbit information, including in aggregated or anonymized form.

## Connected-Service Controls

You may disconnect WHOOP, Fitbit/Google Health, Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, or another supported service from Vivordo's settings. Disconnecting stops future API access and Vivordo deletes its stored access and refresh credentials. Where supported, Vivordo also sends a revocation request to the connected service.

When you disconnect WHOOP, Vivordo lets you choose whether to retain previously imported WHOOP history, including recovery measurements, or delete it. Disconnecting other services does **not** automatically delete measurements or calendar-derived information that Vivordo imported before disconnection. Previously imported information remains in your Vivordo account so that your history and derived trends remain available until you delete the applicable information, delete your Vivordo account, or ask us to delete it. You can also manage or revoke third-party access from the connected service's account settings.

## Data Storage, Security, and International Transfers

Vivordo stores information using service providers that may process it in the United States and other countries. This means information may be transferred outside your province, state, or country and may be subject to the laws of the place where it is processed.

We use safeguards designed to protect information, including encrypted network connections, access controls, server-side storage of connected-account credentials, and encryption at rest provided by our infrastructure providers. OAuth client secrets are not embedded in the app, and connected-service access and refresh tokens are not made available through the app's client database rules. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

If we discover a security incident, we will investigate and provide notices to affected users, regulators, connected-service providers, or others when required by law or applicable platform terms.

## Retention and Deletion

We retain account information and user content while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to provide Vivordo. Imported WHOOP, Fitbit/Google Health, Apple Health, Bluetooth wearable, and other health information is generally retained until you delete the applicable information, delete your account, or ask us to delete it. OAuth credentials are retained only while the relevant connection remains authorized and are deleted when you disconnect it.

You can delete your Vivordo account from **Settings → Delete Account**. Vivordo requires you to authenticate again and confirm the irreversible request. Account deletion removes your Firebase authentication account; Vivordo profile; imported and user-entered health and wellness information; derived scores, burnout results, and insights; daily records; Vivordo AI saved facts and conversation summaries; journal entries; workouts; goals; questionnaires; device and notification records; Circle profile, friendships, comments, likes, activity, and challenge participation; connected-service credentials; and uploaded profile photo. Vivordo also sends supported revocation requests to connected providers, including WHOOP, Google Health/Fitbit, and Sign in with Apple. The process removes your personal participation from shared challenges while preserving the other participants' data.

After a verified deletion request, we delete or de-identify information under our control within the period required by applicable law, except information we must retain for legal compliance, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, security, or enforcement. Information may remain temporarily in encrypted backups until those backups are overwritten through their ordinary lifecycle. We do not use backup copies for ordinary product purposes.

Deleting information from Vivordo does not delete the original information held by Apple, Google, Fitbit, WHOOP, Microsoft, another connected service, or another user. You must use that service's controls to delete information it controls.
